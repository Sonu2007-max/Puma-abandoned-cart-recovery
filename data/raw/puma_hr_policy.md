# PUMA Employee Handbook & People Policy Guidelines
Company: PUMA Sports India Pvt. Ltd. / PUMA SE
Brand Mission: FOREVER. FASTER.
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: Corporate Headquarters, Regional Offices & Retail Operations

---

## 1. PUMA Culture & Workplace Ethos
At PUMA, we are the fastest sports brand in the world. Our culture thrives on high energy, speed, agility, and teamwork. We empower our employees to live an active, athletic lifestyle both on and off the track.

- **Dress Code:** Everyday Sports Casual. Employees are encouraged to wear PUMA apparel, lifestyle sneakers, and activewear in corporate offices. Formal business attire is not required unless meeting external government or statutory delegates.
- **Office Working Hours (Corporate HQ):** Core working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
- **Hybrid Work Policy (Corporate HQ):** PUMA operates on a flexible 3+2 hybrid model: 3 days in-office for collaboration and creative exchange, and up to 2 days remote working per week with prior manager coordination.
- **Retail Store Staff Work Hours:** Retail store associates work in rostered 8.5-hour shifts across a 6-day work week with 1 scheduled rotational weekly off. Overtime and festival shift allowances are paid at 1.5x hourly rate.

## 2. Leave Entitlements & Absence Guidelines

- **Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave:**
  - Corporate and retail staff accrue 1.5 days of EL per completed calendar month (18 days per year).
  - Up to 30 days of unused EL can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.
  - Maximum 15 days of EL can be encashed at the time of separation or retirement based on basic salary.

- **Casual Leave (CL):**
  - Employees receive 12 days of Casual Leave credited on January 1st of each calendar year.
  - CL cannot be accumulated or carried forward; unused CL lapses on December 31st.
  - Maximum 3 consecutive days of CL can be taken at one time.

- **Sick & Wellness Leave (SL):**
  - 10 days of paid Sick Leave are credited annually.
  - Medical certificates from a registered medical practitioner are mandatory for sick leaves extending beyond 3 consecutive working days.

- **PUMA Sports & Marathon Leave (Special Benefit):**
  - In alignment with our sports DNA, every full-time employee receives **3 additional paid days off per year** to participate in recognized marathons, triathlons, national/state sporting tournaments, or official PUMA-sponsored fitness challenges.
  - Prior notification to the People & Culture team with event registration proof is required.

- **Maternity Leave:**
  - Female employees are entitled to **26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave** for up to two surviving children.
  - Can be initiated up to 8 weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
  - Up to 1 month of additional work-from-home can be requested post-maternity upon return.

- **Paternity Leave:**
  - Male employees receive **3 weeks (15 working days) of fully paid paternity leave**, valid for up to 6 months post-childbirth or legal adoption.

- **Bereavement Leave:**
  - Up to 5 consecutive paid working days in the unfortunate event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).

---

## 3. Resignation, Notice Period & Offboarding
- **Corporate Staff:** Notice period is 60 days (2 months). Notice buyout requires VP / People & Culture Head approval.
- **Retail Store Associates:** Notice period is 30 days (1 month).
- **Exit Formalities:** Full and Final Settlement (FnF) is disbursed within 30 days of the last working day following return of company assets, employee discount card deactivation, and clearance from line manager.
