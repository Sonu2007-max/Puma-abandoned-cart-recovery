# PUMA Comprehensive Employee Health, Sports Injury & Wellness Policy
Company: PUMA Sports India Pvt. Ltd. / PUMA SE
Policy Provider: PUMA Corporate HealthShield (Powered by MediBuddy TPA)
Policy Year: 2026-2027

---

## 1. Coverage Limits & Family Floater Structure
- **Sum Insured:** **₹7,50,000 (7.5 Lakhs)** per employee per annum on a family floater basis.
- **Covered Dependents:** Self, legally married Spouse, up to 2 dependent Children (up to age 25), and 2 dependent Parents or Parents-in-law.
- **Room Rent Allowance:** Single private air-conditioned room with no capping on room rent charges within network hospitals. ICU/CCU charges covered at actuals.
- **Co-Payment:** 0% co-payment for employee, spouse, and children. A nominal 10% co-pay applies strictly to dependent parent hospital claims.

---

## 2. Specialized Sports Injury & Orthopedic Care (PUMA Athletic Advantage)
Because PUMA employees actively engage in running, football, training, and athletic pursuits, our corporate policy includes dedicated sports injury protection:
- **Sports Injury Treatment:** 100% cashless hospitalization coverage for sports-related injuries, including ACL/PCL tears, meniscus tears, Achilles tendon ruptures, fractures, and rotator cuff injuries sustained during recreational or competitive sports.
- **Rehabilitation & Post-Operative Physiotherapy:** Up to **₹50,000 per year** covered for prescribed sports physiotherapy, physical rehab, and chiropractic sessions following a sports injury or surgery.
- **Orthopedic Implants & Arthroscopy:** Fully covered on actual medical bills without arbitrary sub-limits.

---

## 3. General In-Patient & Daycare Procedures
- **Pre-Hospitalization:** Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days prior to hospital admission (pathology tests, doctor consultations, MRI/CT scans) are reimbursable.
- **Post-Hospitalization:** Prescribed follow-up medicines and consultant fees up to 90 days following discharge are reimbursable.
- **Day Care Procedures:** Over 540 modern daycare procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization (cataract surgery, chemotherapy, dialysis, minor orthopedic keyhole surgeries) are fully covered.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** **Waived completely (0 days)** for all full-time PUMA employees and their dependents from Day 1 of joining.

---

## 4. Maternity & Newborn Benefits
- **Maternity Normal Delivery:** Up to **₹1,00,000** covered.
- **Maternity Caesarean (C-Section):** Up to **₹1,50,000** covered.
- **Maternity Waiting Period:** **Zero waiting period**; coverage commences immediately upon employment confirmation.
- **Newborn Baby Cover:** Covered from Day 1 of birth within the overall family floater sum insured up to 90 days without waiting for policy renewal endorsement.

---

## 5. Annual Fitness, Gym & Wellness Allowance
- **Annual Fitness Reimbursement:** Every full-time PUMA employee is entitled to **₹25,000 per financial year** toward fitness club memberships (Anytime Fitness, Cult.fit, Gold's Gym), personal athletic training, marathon registration fees, or sports club passes.
- **Claim Process:** Submit tax invoice and payment proof on the PUMA Wellness portal by the 20th of each month.
- **Mental Wellbeing (PUMA MindCare):** Unlimited, 100% confidential 24/7 tele-counseling sessions for employees and family members with certified psychologists and wellness therapists.

---

## 6. Exclusions
- **Routine Dental Care:** General dental checkups, cleaning, braces, and veneers are excluded. Dental treatment necessitated by accidental sports injury or facial impact is covered.
- **Cosmetic Surgeries:** Elective aesthetic treatments not required for medical reconstructive purposes are excluded.
- **Alternative Therapies:** AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga, Homeopathy) is covered up to ₹50,000 annually only if administered in a government-recognized hospital.

---

## 7. Claim Procedures: Cashless & Reimbursement
- **Cashless Hospitalization (Preferred):**
  - Planned admission: Submit pre-authorization request via the MediBuddy app or hospital TPA desk at least **48 hours in advance**.
  - Emergency admission: Intimate TPA desk within **24 hours of hospital admission**. Show digital PUMA Health e-Card and government photo ID.
- **Reimbursement Claims:**
  - Submit original bills, discharge summary, diagnostic reports, and completed claim form within **30 days of hospital discharge**.
  - Reimbursement turnaround time (TAT): 7 to 10 working days directly into salary bank account.
