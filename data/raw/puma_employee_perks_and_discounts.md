# PUMA Employee Perks, Product Discounts & Athletic Sponsorship Policy
Company: PUMA Sports India Pvt. Ltd. / PUMA SE
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies to: All Permanent Employees (Corporate, Regional & Retail Teams)

---

## 1. PUMA Employee Store & Online Discount
At PUMA, we want our team to be our biggest brand ambassadors. All active employees enjoy significant discounts on our athletic and lifestyle product lines:
- **Employee Store Discount:** **Flat 50% discount** on Maximum Retail Price (MRP) across all PUMA Exclusive Brand Stores (EBOs) in India and the official `puma.com` employee portal.
- **Monthly Purchase Cap:** Up to **₹40,000 gross MRP per month** (meaning an employee pays maximum ₹20,000 out-of-pocket after the 50% discount).
- **Applicability:** Valid on all footwear (Nitro running, football boots, motorsport, classic Suede/Palermo), apparel, and accessories. Excludes limited-edition hyper-exclusive artist drops (e.g. Fenty / Rihanna, Select Collabs) during the first 14 days of release.
- **Discount Card / ID:** Activated upon joining via the PUMA Employee Perks App using official work email.

---

## 2. Annual Footwear Allowance & Gear Kits
- **Annual Footwear Allocation:** Full-time corporate and retail employees receive **2 complimentary pairs of performance running or training shoes per financial year** (one pair in April, one pair in October).
- **Annual Apparel Voucher:** Employees receive a **₹15,000 digital voucher** annually to purchase PUMA training gear, gym wear, and jackets.
- **Retail Store Uniform:** Retail associates receive 3 complimentary sets of current-season PUMA tracksuits, t-shirts, and sneakers every 6 months to wear on store duty.

---

## 3. Friends & Family (F&F) Passes
- Every employee receives **4 Friends & Family discount passes per calendar year**.
- Each pass grants a **30% discount on MRP** for up to a single transaction value of ₹25,000.
- Passes can be gifted to friends, relatives, or acquaintances through the PUMA MyPerks portal.

---

## 4. PUMA Athlete Sponsorship & Marathon Support
We actively sponsor employees who compete in athletic events and marathons:
- **Event Registration Subsidy:** PUMA reimburses 100% of registration entry fees for authorized major marathons (e.g., Tata Mumbai Marathon, Vedanta Delhi Half Marathon, Bengaluru 10K, TCS World 10K) and national sporting events.
- **Race Day Gear Kit:** Employees registered for an approved marathon receive a complimentary **PUMA NITRO Race Day Kit** (PUMA Deviate Nitro or Fast-R running shoes, dryCELL racing singlet, and hydration gear).
- **Podium Cash Bonus:** Employees who finish in the top 3 of any open/veteran category in a national-level race receive a special recognition bonus of ₹25,000.

---

## 5. PUMA Corporate Headquarters Amenities
For employees working at our corporate headquarters in Bangalore / regional hubs:
- **In-Office PUMA Fitness Center:** State-of-the-art gym with functional fitness zones, treadmills, free weights, and shower facilities open from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- **Healthy Nutrition Cafe:** Subsidized sports nutrition cafe serving high-protein meals, fresh smoothies, cold-pressed juices, and healthy snacks.
- **Weekly Fitness Sessions:** Free weekly on-campus yoga, HIIT, cross-training, and football club matches organized by certified trainers.
