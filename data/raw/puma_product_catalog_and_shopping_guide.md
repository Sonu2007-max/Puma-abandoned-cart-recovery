## 2. Customer Objections & "Why You Did Not Buy" Solutions

When customers hesitate or drop out before purchasing, address their specific hesitation immediately with these policy-backed solutions:

### Objection 1: "Price is too high / Expensive / Out of budget"
- **Root Reason:** Customer perceives high initial expenditure without comparing longevity or technology benefits.
- **PUMA Solution:**
  1. Highlight cost-per-kilometer value: Deviate Nitro lasts 800+ km (approx ₹16 per run).
  2. Provide instant exclusive WhatsApp recovery coupon: **`PUMAFAST15`** (Flat 15% OFF) or **`NITRO20`** (Flat 20% OFF on select performance shoes).
  3. Offer No-Cost EMI: 3 months at ₹2,999/month on credit/debit cards and UPI PayLater.
  4. Suggest budget-friendly alternative: e.g., recommend Velocity Nitro 3 (₹7,499) as an alternative to Deviate Nitro (₹13,599).

### Objection 2: "Unsure about size / What if it doesn't fit my feet?"
- **Root Reason:** Fear of buying online without trying on; wide feet concerns.
- **PUMA Solution:**
  1. Provide exact UK/India size conversion chart and centimetre measurements.
  2. Standard PUMA runs true to UK sizing. For Nitro running shoes, wide-foot runners should order 0.5 size larger.
  3. Reassure with **Free Doorstep Size Exchange**: PUMA courier delivers the replacement size to their doorstep and picks up the old pair simultaneously at ₹0 extra fee.

### Objection 3: "Just looking / Need time to think / Will buy later"
- **Root Reason:** Procrastination, lack of urgency, cart abandonment.
- **PUMA Solution:**
  1. Create soft urgency: Remind them that popular sizes (UK 8 & 9) have limited stock (often less than 5 pairs in warehouse).
  2. Offer to hold their size for 24 hours with an exclusive reserved checkout link.
  3. Send an instant one-click WhatsApp pay/order link.

### Objection 4: "Are these 100% genuine / Can I return if I don't like them?"
- **Root Reason:** Trust issues, fear of counterfeit products or difficult return processes.
- **PUMA Solution:**
  1. 100% Genuine Direct-from-Brand Guarantee with official manufacturer invoice.
  2. **30-Day Hassle-Free Returns**: Full refund to original payment source or UPI within 48 hours of return pickup. No questions asked.
  3. Cash on Delivery (COD) available with open-box verification support at delivery.

### Objection 5: "Which shoe is right for me? (Confusion between models)"
- **Root Reason:** Choice overload between Deviate Nitro, Velocity Nitro, and Palermo.
- **PUMA Solution:**
  - Fast quiz: Ask distance & goal.
    - If 10K/Half-Marathon race pace -> **Deviate Nitro 2**
    - If daily 5K jog / gym / walking -> **Velocity Nitro 3**
    - If everyday college / casual fashion -> **Palermo or Suede Classic**
